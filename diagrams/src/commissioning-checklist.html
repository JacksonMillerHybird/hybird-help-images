Hybird commissioning checklist

Installer

1Before power

Power off, or work under the live-working rules of DS/EN 50110.

  • PSU, UPS (if fitted) and controller mounted, left to right: PSU → UPS → controller
  • Power wiring correctly terminated and secure
  • RS485 daisy chain complete: no star wiring, no T‑taps
  • A/B polarity the same at the controller and every device
  • Repeater fitted if the bus has more than 70 breakers
  • Ethernet ready where possible; Wi-Fi as fallback

2Power on

Breakers must be powered to be addressed. Take the necessary precautions.

  • Controller green power LED on
  • Controller online in Hybird OS
  • Detach every RCD and meter from the RS485 bus, then tap I Detached Every RCD and Meter

3Each breaker, in the Assembly guide

Breakers arrive in Setup mode (green dip switch down).

  • Scan Device QR (or Enter URL Manually…)
  • Flip Green Switch to Setup · purple in the LED pattern
  • Write Address to Register
  • Flip Green Switch to Work · LED blinks one colour
  • Run Device Test

Only one breaker in Setup mode at a time.

4RCDs and meters

  • Address each RCD on its own: it is the only device connected to the controller RS485 bus
  • Each RCD has 230 V on A1/A2 as well as its bottom supply
  • Meters: pick the meter model, Isolate This Meter, Run Device Test
  • Every RCD and meter reconnected to the bus when the guide asks

5Check the whole bus

  • Test the Bus, then Confirm Addressing
  • Every breaker LED blinks one colour: red (on) or blue (off), no purple
  • Every device reports in Hybird OS
  • Each breaker labelled with its circuit name
  • Switching checked on one non-critical circuit

6Hand over

  • Build complete and every device reporting
  • Move to Operating only now: it permanently locks the panel to its current account and site

iIf something is wrong

  • One breaker silent: check its A/B wires and plug
  • None reporting: check A/B at the controller
  • A group silent: look for a break after the last reporting device
  • Stuck? Ask in the Messenger at help.hybird.energy
